Dropbox sync

Backs up Continue Watching, Watchlist, and settings to a single file in your Dropbox's app folder, and merges them back in (newest wins) on every device running this extension.

  1. Create a free app at dropbox.com/developers/apps with Scoped access and the App folder access type.
  2. Under Permissions, enable files.content.write and files.content.read, then click Submit.
  3. Under OAuth 2 → Redirect URIs, add the URI shown below, then paste the app's App key here and click Connect.

New release tracking & Notifications

Monitors TMDB for new TV episode air dates and sends a desktop notification when a new episode of something in your Watchlist is released.

Uncheck any show to disable release tracking and notifications for that specific title.

Homepage discovery rails

Multi-row discovery powered by TMDB (same free key as above): personalized “Because you watched…”, Now Playing, Trending Movies/TV, Popular TV, Anime Spotlight, and genre rows. Your Continue Watching and Watchlist rails always stay first. Clicking a poster opens the best Nepu match when possible.
